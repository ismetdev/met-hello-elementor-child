Tenders and careers lists on phone: mockup

Stage 1 of 3. This is a picture of the plan, not the final code. Nothing is changed on any site. Open it on your phone or a desktop browser. The filters and search work, so you can try them.

What you are looking at
  • Phone (375px) and iPad portrait (768px): each row of the table becomes a card. No sideways scroll.
  • Desktop (1000px): the table stays as it is today. It is shown for reference only.
  • Titles, reference numbers, issuers and locations come from the local copy of the V2 site (the sample tenders and jobs there). Closing dates are moved so that every state appears: open, closing soon (7 days or less) and closed. One tender has a long title of 200 characters. One tender has an excerpt, written for this mockup.
  • The live pages could not be reached from the build machine, so nothing was copied from them.

1. Tenders

Cards: reference and status first, then the title, issuer and place, then the closing date with the document button. The tender with no document shows "Details". Try the tabs and the search box.

2. Careers

Cards: status and closing date first, then the position, company and type, then department and location.

3. Empty result (new message)

Today the "No tenders found." and "No positions found." messages only appear when there are no records at all. They do not appear when a search or tab has no match. The mockup shows the message for both. It also shows on desktop.

4. Desktop (unchanged)

At 769px and wider the table stays. Only the empty message is added.
